# KRITI NEET 2027 — OFFICIAL DATA POLICY
## Authoritative Government Sources & Grounded Truth Invariants

### 1. Fundamental Principle: Zero Unverified Data
The NTA Notifications & Official Exam Intelligence Center operates under a strict, non-negotiable rule:
- **No Dummy Data**
- **No Fake Data**
- **No Fabricated Notifications**
- **No AI-Generated Rumors**
- **No Predictions Presented as Official Facts**
- **No Social Media / Coaching Claims as Official Truth**

---

### 2. Tier 1 Official Government Authorities
Only three official government entities have legal competence to establish NEET UG examination rules:

1. **National Testing Agency (NTA)**
   - Official Domains: `nta.ac.in`, `neet.nta.nic.in`
   - Authority Scope: Public notices, examination schedules, admit cards, information bulletins, city intimations, application windows, correction facilities, answer keys, scorecards.

2. **National Medical Commission (NMC) / Under Graduate Medical Education Board (UGMEB)**
   - Official Domain: `nmc.org.in`
   - Authority Scope: Statutory syllabus finalization, eligibility requirements, admission regulations.

---

### 3. Role of AI
- AI is strictly restricted to extraction, classification, and grounded summarization assistance.
- **AI never establishes official facts.**
- Every AI summary must be labeled as `AI SUMMARY • Grounded on Official Source` with traceable evidence citation.
- If AI summarization fails or is unavailable, the unedited official government publication is displayed directly.

---

### 4. Separation of Current vs Historical Information
- Historical NEET 2026 / 2025 notices are explicitly labeled `HISTORICAL ARCHIVE`.
- Rules from NEET 2026 are never assumed to apply to NEET 2027 unless a subsequent official notification carries them forward.
- If NEET 2027 exam date or mode has not been announced by NTA, the system explicitly states:
  > **"NEET 2027 exam date has not yet been officially announced."**
  > **"Current Officially Confirmed Mode: Not yet officially announced."**
